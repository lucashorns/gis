Little Cottonwood Canyon Virtual Gondola Tower Tour

This virtual tour showcases the locations and heights of the 22 gondola towers approved by the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) for construction in Little Cottonwood Canyon.

While the 3D renderings are conceptual and do not represent the final tower designs, the tower heights are accurate, offering a realistic sense of how the structures will appear within the landscape. We relied on UDOT’s public plans, which is why our renderings do not include cables, cars, the two angle stations, the base station and stops at Snowbird and Alta resorts, as those details are not publicly available.

Click on a tower site to learn more about that location, or start the guided tour to visit each tower one by one as you take a virtual "drive" up the canyon.
